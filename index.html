Background

Hello, my name is Maria Robinson and my primary research interest is in combining empirical work and formal modeling to study attention, memory and higher-level cognition, such as decision-making and metacognition.

I'm also interested in topics on best practices in measurement and theory assessment in psychology, and am part of the Theory, Model, Measurement network founded by Dr. Kenny Yu and Dr. Niels Vanhasbroeck.
